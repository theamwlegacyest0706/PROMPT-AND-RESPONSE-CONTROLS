THE AMW LEGACY
CONTROLLED POLICIES, STANDARDS & SAFEGUARDS
Print-ready controlled reference • Source-preserving edition • August 30, 2026
This manual consolidates AMW policies, standards, safeguards, and locked controls already established in the controlled record. It does not use retired legal or ownership language as current policy.
1. AMW Readiness Focus
AMW does not confuse urgency with rushing. We reduce unnecessary rework, close responsibilities that are already substantially complete, and concentrate research and resources on dependencies that prevent later responsibilities from moving forward.
2. Application Continuity Rule
When AMW or any Partner has already established and verified an answer relevant to a later intake, application, or form, as well as Founders, CEO, CFO, CMO, CTO, and Chair of the Board, AMW carries that answer forward and elaborates only as necessary for the new question. AMW does not repeatedly reopen settled facts unless new information, conflicting evidence, or a changed requirement creates a reason to do so.
3. Current-Form First Rule
Before drafting or answering an intake or application, locate the current form or authoritative requirements whenever reasonably available; prepare against the actual questions.
4. Verification Control
AMW verifies before correcting. AMW double-checks and handles what it can internally before outsourcing.
5. Partner Research Continuity Control
AMW will always retrieve what we've established, inspect the authoritative source, verify before challenging a factual lead, and return with the unresolved issue—not make the Founder rebuild the record.
6. Apparent-Ambiguity Research Gate
Research apparent ambiguity before elevating it to the Founder. If research resolves the issue, return with the verified answer. If the issue remains genuinely unresolved, return with the issue and the evidence.
7. Service-Description Fit Rule
Use the level of service detail appropriate to the form or context being completed.
8. Current-Status Naming Rule
Use the current legal or official name and status. A planned LLC, trade name, or public identity is not represented as legally established before the relevant authority establishes it.
9. Self-Explaining Stewardship Standard
Operational instruments should allow a reasonably prepared Steward to understand what the instrument is for, what information belongs there, what decision follows, and where responsibility goes next without depending upon unwritten institutional memory.
10. Founder Recognition & Attribution Control
AMW will not omit, diminish, reclassify, or inaccurately attribute an established Founder, Owner, Creator, Contributor, or institutional role merely because another person performs more visible work, communicates more frequently, serves as an applicant or presenter, or appears in public-facing materials. Before producing any biography, application, grant narrative, governance document, organizational description, pitch, website content, contract, or institutional history, AMW must cross-reference the controlled record for established ownership, Founder status, contribution, attribution, and role distinctions.
11. Co-Founder Status Control
Alicia Walker and Indya Massey are Co-Founders. Neither Founder's legitimacy depends upon equal hours, identical responsibilities, public visibility, or participation in a particular AMW activity.
12. Closed Institutional Facts Control
Closed institutional facts are not research subjects. Retrieve the current controlled answer first, exclude retired material, and research only a genuinely unresolved issue.
13. No Forced Revalidation Rule
Settled facts are not repeatedly forced back onto the Founder for proof when AMW already possesses the controlled answer.
14. Durable Artifact Control
Completed conversational or controlled work counts as completed AMW work. Substantive developing work also remains AMW work. Material work is made durable, visual, downloadable, and filed according to its status.
15. No False Completeness Control
Do not describe a summary, index, architecture record, preservation bundle, or partially accessible compilation as the complete Legacy or Complete AMOS unless the actual underlying work has been materialized and verified.
16. Ordinary-Work Artifact Discipline
When materially completing or updating an instrument, create or update its durable artifact at that time rather than waiting for the Friday review.
17. Preservation Workflow
Build → Lock/Status → Create or update durable artifact → File by status → Add to Master Index → Friday verify/consolidate.
18. Minimum Necessary Information Rule
AMW explains why information is necessary before collecting it and limits collection to what is reasonably needed for the responsibility.
19. Funding Classification Control
Loans are not AMW funding and will never be represented as grants. AMW's own active funding search excludes debt and ownership dilution.
20. Funding Headline / Terms Distinction
Review controlling eligibility, application, award, use, reporting, repayment, publicity, and ownership terms before describing a resource as unrestricted, nonrepayable, no-cost, ownership-free, or usable.
21. Application Representation Rule
Information entered in an application shows what AMW intended or represented; it does not by itself prove that an authority accepted, approved, or legally created the represented condition.
22. Inactivity Evidence Rule
The absence of revenue, services, transactions, capital contribution, operational participation, or completed ownership transfer is preserved as evidence relevant to determining the legal effect of a person's historical listing; AMW does not treat business inactivity alone as conclusive proof of ownership or nonownership.
23. Correction Evidence Rule
AMW uses a Certificate of Correction only when the underlying filing, original provision, filing date, claimed error, corrected provision, and required execution can be identified and supported. AMW does not use a correction instrument to rewrite a transaction that actually occurred.
24. Institutional Readiness Cost Principle
AMW does not delay becoming institutionally ready merely because it cannot presently afford every readiness cost. AMW identifies the requirement, preserves the cost, searches for lawful resources capable of meeting it, and does not represent the requirement as satisfied until it actually is.
25. Daily Practice Standard
Leave the responsibility no worse—and whenever reasonably possible, better.
26. Practice Principle
Prepare, Teach, Practice and Try Again.
27. Institutional Learning Return Standard
Institutional learning is not complete when AMW understands something; it is complete when AMW responsibly does something with what it learned and makes the resulting value available to the institution.
28. Learning Return Rule
No learning is complete until it has had an opportunity to return to the place where it can create value.
29. Interface Threshold Question
What does responsible Stewardship require at this threshold?
30. Interface Return Verification
Did we close what could responsibly be closed without abandoning what still needs to be carried?
31. Exception Trace Rule
Temporary authority should leave no permanent shadow, and an exception should never outlive the reason that made it responsible.
32. Three-Room Mirror Test
Can a person, relationship, information matter, or disruption cross AMW's institutional threshold without losing dignity, context, responsibility, protection, accountability, learning, or a path home?
33. Resource Allocation / Promise Control
Resource Promise and Resource Possession remain separate responsibilities. A resource is not treated as available for allocation merely because it has been discussed, promised, requested, or expected.
34. Weekly Continuity Review
Compare the active controlled record against current documents, applications, pipelines, deadlines, credential records, and retired drafts so obsolete language does not re-enter current work.
