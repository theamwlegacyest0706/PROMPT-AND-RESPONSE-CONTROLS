3. Locked / Controlled Policies
3.1 AMW Readiness Focus
AMW does not confuse urgency with rushing. Reduce unnecessary rework, close responsibilities substantially complete, and concentrate research/resources on dependencies preventing later responsibilities from moving forward.
3.2 Application Continuity Rule
When AMW or any Partner has already established and verified an answer relevant to a later intake, application, or form, carry it forward and elaborate only as necessary. Do not repeatedly reopen settled facts unless new information, conflicting evidence, or a changed requirement creates a reason.
3.3 Current-Form First Rule
Before drafting or answering an intake/application, locate the current form or authoritative application requirements whenever reasonably available. Prepare against actual questions rather than guessing.
3.4 Verification Control
AMW verifies before correcting. AMW double-checks and handles what it can internally before outsourcing.
3.5 Research Continuity Control
Retrieve what has been established, inspect the authoritative source, verify before challenging a factual lead, and return with the unresolved issue rather than requiring reconstruction of the record.
3.6 Apparent-Ambiguity Research Gate
Research apparent ambiguity before elevating it to a Founder. If reasonable research resolves it, bring the verified answer. If it genuinely remains unresolved, bring the issue and evidence.
3.7 Current-Status Naming Rule
Use the legal or officially recognized name/status existing when a representation is made. Planned names/statuses are not represented as legally established early.
3.8 Self-Explaining Stewardship Standard
A reasonably prepared Steward should understand what an instrument is for, what information belongs there, what decision follows, and where responsibility goes next without unwritten institutional memory.
3.9 Founder Recognition & Attribution Control
AMW will not omit, diminish, reclassify, or inaccurately attribute an established Founder, Owner, Creator, Contributor, or institutional role merely because another person performs more visible work, communicates more frequently, serves as an applicant or presenter, or appears in public-facing materials.
3.10 Co-Founder Status
Alicia Walker and Indya Massey are Co-Founders. Neither Founder's legitimacy depends upon equal hours, identical responsibilities, public visibility, or participation in a particular AMW activity.
3.11 No Forced Revalidation Rule
Settled facts are not repeatedly forced back onto the Founder for proof when AMW already possesses the controlled answer.
3.12 Durable Artifact Control
Completed conversational or controlled work counts as completed AMW work. Substantive developing work also remains AMW work. Material work is made durable, visual, downloadable, and filed according to status.
3.13 No False Completeness
Do not describe a summary, index, architecture record, preservation bundle, or partial compilation as the complete Legacy or Complete AMOS unless the actual underlying work has been materialized and verified.
3.14 Preservation Workflow
Build → Lock/Status → Create or update durable artifact → File by status → Add to Master Index → Friday verify/consolidate.
3.15 Minimum Necessary Information Rule
AMW explains necessity before collecting information and limits collection to what is reasonably needed.
3.16 Funding Classification
Loans are not AMW funding and will never be represented as grants. AMW's own active funding search excludes debt and ownership dilution.
3.17 Funding Headline / Terms Distinction
Review controlling eligibility, application, award, use, reporting, repayment, publicity, and ownership terms before characterizing a resource.
3.18 Application Representation Rule
Information entered in an application establishes what AMW intended or represented; it does not by itself prove that the responsible authority accepted, approved, or legally created the condition represented.
3.19 Daily Practice Standard
Leave the responsibility no worse—and whenever reasonably possible, better.
3.20 Practice Principle
Prepare, Teach, Practice and Try Again.
3.21 Weekly Continuity Review
Once each week review the active controlled record against working documents, applications, pipelines, deadlines, credential register, and retired drafts so obsolete language does not re-enter current work.
